---
id: C3-duplicate
expect: REJECT
criterion: "Criterion 3 — necessity, restates an existing rule"
why: Re-proposes a rule the protocol already states in its hard rules.
---
### Area
Principles

### Is this evidence or preference?
Evidence — something was actually lost

### What happened
A round needed a benchmark figure it could not produce, because the benchmark
requires a paid service the project has no credentials for. The writeup reported a
figure anyway, derived by reasoning from a similar published system. Two rounds
later the credential arrived, the benchmark ran, and the real figure was materially
different. Two documents had quoted the reasoned figure in the meantime.

### What it cost
Two documents carrying a wrong number for two rounds, and a queue ordered against
it. Correcting them took most of a round.

### How often
Once

### Rounds run
7

### Proposed change
Add a principle stating that measurements must come from execution:

> **Numbers come from runs.** A figure that appears in a writeup, a document, or a
> state file must be the output of something that actually executed in that round.
> A figure derived by reasoning, however careful, is not a measurement and must not
> be recorded as one.

### Blast radius
None for projects that already only publish measured numbers — it describes what
they already do. Projects that estimate lose the ability to.

### Why this might be wrong
This may be close enough to something already in the protocol that adding it creates
two statements of one rule, which drift. If so, the right change is a pointer rather
than a new principle.
