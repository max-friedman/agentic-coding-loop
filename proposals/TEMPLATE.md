# NNN — <one line naming the problem, not the solution>

## What happened

The concrete sequence. Which step of the protocol was involved, and what the agent
or the human actually did. No repository names, file paths, code, or business
specifics — describe the shape of the problem.

## What it cost

The wasted work, the missed catch, the wrong turn taken. Be specific: "re-derived a
decision from round 2 and spent the session on it" is a cost. "Felt awkward" is a
preference — which is fine, but say so.

## How often

Once, or every round? A one-off is worth reporting. A pattern is worth changing the
protocol for. Say which this is, and how many rounds you have run.

## Proposed change

The narrowest edit that would have prevented it. Quote the current text and the
replacement where you can.

If this **adds** a step or a section, say which one it replaces — or argue why the
protocol is genuinely missing one. Every line here is read in full, every round, by
every project.

## Blast radius

What this costs a project that never had your problem. Who it slows down. What it
would break for someone relying on the current behavior.

## Why this might be wrong

The strongest argument against your own proposal. If you cannot construct one, the
proposal is probably a preference wearing evidence's clothes — which is worth
knowing before review rather than after.
